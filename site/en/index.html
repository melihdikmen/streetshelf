Gift

Gift it

Something you no longer use can go to a neighbor instead of the bin.

  • You can also post what you need as "wanted".

Lend

Lend it

Lend the drill for a week and get it back. Nobody should have to buy something they need once a year.

  • Your neighbor picks the dates, you accept.
  • The handover and the return are recorded with photos.
Rent

Rent it out

Set a daily price and let your things earn while they would sit idle.

  • Payment is cash at the handover.
  • No money moves through the app, and there is no commission.

How it works

Every step from listing to handover happens in the app.

  1. Post or browse

    Photograph your item and describe it, or look at listings nearby.

  2. Pick dates, request

    Send a request with a short note; your neighbor accepts.

  3. Meet through the chat

    Agree on where to meet in the in-app chat.

  4. Photograph the handover

    Photograph the handover and the return, then review each other.

Frequently asked questions

How do I borrow something from a neighbor?

Browse listings near you or in your group, pick the dates on the item you want and send a request with a short note. Once your neighbor accepts, you agree on where to meet in the in-app chat. The handover and the return are recorded with photos.

Is StreetShelf free?

Yes, the app is free to use, with no membership or commission. Gifting and lending are completely free. For rentals, the owner sets the daily price.

Can other people see my location?

No. Your exact address or location is never shown. Others only see an approximate distance and an area of roughly 100 meters on the map.

How do I pay for a rental?

The price is paid in cash at the handover. The app takes no payments and moves no money.
